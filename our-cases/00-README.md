## 1. Autonomous Networks Manifesto — Signatory (2023)

Telecom Argentina is one of the **31 original signatories** of TM Forum's Autonomous Networks Manifesto, launched at DTW23 Ignite in Copenhagen.

**Full list of signatories:**
Accenture, AIS, Amdocs, China Mobile, China Telecom, Chunghwa Telecom, Ericsson, Futurewei, Huawei, IBM, Inspur, Intraway, Iquall Networks, Makman Technology Consulting, Microsoft, MTN Group, Netcracker, Nokia, NTT, Orange, SAS, stc, **Telecom Argentina**, Telefonica, Telenor, Telkomsel, TTG International, UBiqube, Verizon, Wipro, and Zain.

**What the manifesto commits to:**
- Enhance network resilience, agility, and efficiency
- Adopt TM Forum standards and best practices for AN
- Underpin a new wave of telecoms growth
- Industry-wide commitment to autonomous operations

**Source:** [TM Forum Press Release](https://www.tmforum.org/press-and-news/30-major-csps-and-vendors-commit-to-tm-forums-standards-and-best-practice-to-speed-the-adoption-of-autonomous-networks/)

**Note:** Intraway and Iquall Networks (both Argentine companies) are also signatories, suggesting a local ecosystem around AN.

## 3. Huawei Partnership — AI & Intelligent Transformation

### Huawei Xinghe AI Ultra-Resilient Fabric Deployment (2025)
- **What:** Deployment of Huawei's Xinghe AI Ultra-Resilient Fabric across Telecom Argentina's data center network
- **Scale:** 2 national + 6 regional data centers, distances exceeding 200 km
- **Challenges solved:**
  - Multi-DC configuration delivery (10% manual error rate eliminated)
  - Agile service O&M (reduced expert dependency)
  - Device reliability and flexibility for B2B expansion
- **Results:**
  - iMaster NCE controller managing 1600+ devices
  - Application rollout: from 3 to 10+ per week
  - Stability improved 10x
  - Investment reduced 50% (single device covers GE to 400GE)
  - End-to-end automation across heterogeneous networks
- **Source:** [Huawei Carrier](https://carrier.huawei.com/en/News/ai-wan-Argentina)

### CTO Talks at MWC 2023
