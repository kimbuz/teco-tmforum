# Autonomous Networks Levels (L0–L5)

## Definition

TM Forum defines six levels of network autonomy that describe the progression from fully manual operations to fully autonomous networks. Similar to autonomous driving levels, they measure how much human intervention is required.

---

## The Six Levels

| Level | Name | Human Role | Network Capability |
|-------|------|------------|-------------------|
| **L0** | Manual Operations | Executes everything | None — all tasks are human-driven |
| **L1** | Assisted Operations | Executes all tasks | System provides information and suggestions |
| **L2** | Partial Autonomous | Makes decisions, supervises | System executes specific tasks under human control |
| **L3** | Conditional Autonomous | Oversees, intervenes when needed | System decides and executes within specific domains |
| **L4** | Highly Autonomous | Monitors, handles exceptions | System autonomously manages across multiple domains |
| **L5** | Fully Autonomous | None required | Complete end-to-end autonomy, no human intervention |

---

## Key Transitions

### L2 → L3: From "Human Decides" to "System Decides (in a domain)"
- System starts making decisions, not just executing
- Limited to specific, well-defined domains
- Human still oversees and can override

### L3 → L4: From "Domain Autonomy" to "Cross-Domain Autonomy"
- The critical shift: true autonomous decision-making
- System operates across multiple domains without asking
- Human role shifts from "overseer" to "exception handler"
- **This is where most industry effort is focused (2025–2026)**

### L4 → L5: From "Mostly Autonomous" to "Fully Autonomous"
- End-to-end autonomy across ALL domains
- No human intervention required for any operation
- Business-level autonomous decisions
- **Future target — no operator has achieved this yet**

---

## Assessment: ANLET

TM Forum uses the **Autonomous Network Levels Evaluation Tool (ANLET)** to certify operators at specific levels within specific domains.

Key points:
- Certification is **per domain** (you can be L4 in RAN but L2 in transport)
- Requires evidence of production capabilities
- TM Forum presented 37 certifications in November 2025
- Operators like MasOrange, Ooredoo Kuwait have achieved L4 in specific domains

---

## Industry Status (2025–2026)

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Most CSPs today | Between L2 and L3 |
| 20% of operators | Have reached L4/L5 in select domains |
| 35% of operators | Expect L4/L5 within two years |
| Industry target | Widespread L4 by 2027 |

---

## What Each Level Enables

| Level | Zero Wait | Zero Touch | Zero Trouble |
|-------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| L0 | Long waits | All manual | Reactive only |
| L1 | Reduced | Some assistance | Faster detection |
| L2 | Shorter | Partial automation | Some proactive |
| L3 | Near-real-time | Domain automation | Predictive in domains |
| L4 | Near-instant | Minimal human touch | Proactive cross-domain |
| L5 | **Zero** | **Zero** | **Zero** |
