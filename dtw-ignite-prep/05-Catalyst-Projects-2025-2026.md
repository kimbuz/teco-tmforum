# TM Forum Catalyst Projects — AI & Autonomous Networks (2025–2026)

## What Are Catalysts?

Catalysts are TM Forum's proof-of-concept innovation projects where members collaborate to demonstrate solutions to real industry challenges. They are showcased at DTW events and represent the cutting edge of telecom innovation.

---

## Active Catalyst Projects Related to AI & Autonomous Networks

### Agentic AI Catalysts

#### 1. Agent Fabric — Phase II
- **ID:** C25.0.798
- **Focus:** Multi-vendor ecosystem of autonomous agents across network domains
- **Description:** Enables collaborative agents that work together across network domains instead of isolated scripts or siloed intelligence. Orchestrates specialized agents across different network domains.
- **Link:** [Agent Fabric Phase II](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/C25.0.798/agent-fabric-phase-ii)

#### 2. Agentic & Autonomous AI for Business Excellence
- **ID:** C25.0.816
- **Focus:** Energy optimization and network resource utilization
- **Description:** Minimizing energy consumption and maximizing network resource utilization in demanding scenarios, while generating new revenues.
- **Link:** [Agentic & Autonomous AI](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/C25.0.816/agentic-autonomous-ai-for-business-excellence)

#### 3. Agentic ODA for Proactive Customer Experiences
- **ID:** M25.0.793
- **Focus:** AI-powered ODA components as independent agents
- **Description:** Enhancing ODA with agentic RAG capabilities so each component functions as an independent, AI-enabled agent. Agents communicate across systems, channels, and business domains autonomously.
- **Link:** [Agentic ODA](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/M25.0.793/agentic-oda-for-proactive-customer-experiences)

#### 4. Growing B2B with Autonomous Agents
- **ID:** C25.0.797
- **Focus:** B2B sales transformation
- **Description:** Transforming B2B sales with autonomous agents enabling flexible quoting and commerce for mid-tier enterprise customers.
- **Link:** [Growing B2B](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/C25.0.797/growing-b2b-with-autonomous-agents)

#### 5. Agentic Intelligence Exchange
- **ID:** C25.5.881
- **Focus:** Federated AI marketplace
- **Description:** A federated AI marketplace where telcos, enterprises, and partners co-develop and deploy intelligent agents trained on local data, triggered by real-time events, and orchestrated through standardized Open APIs.
- **Link:** [Agentic Intelligence Exchange](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/C25.5.881/agentic-intelligence-exchange-a-federated-ai-marketplace-for-monetizing-telco-intelligence-in-real-time)

#### 6. Agentic Network Growth Engine
- **ID:** C26.0.925
- **Focus:** Scaling commercial networks with agentic AI
- **Description:** Addresses the inability of human-dependent operations and legacy OSS/BSS architectures to sustainably scale commercial networks.
- **Link:** [Agentic Network Growth Engine](https://s16705.pcdn.co/catalysts/projects/C26.0.925/agentic-network-growth-engine)

### Autonomous Networks Catalysts

#### 7. L4 Autonomous Networks: Agent-Powered Zero-Touch Workflows
- **ID:** M25.0.832
- **Focus:** Level 4 autonomous networks through agent-led workflows
- **Description:** Advances Level 4 autonomous networks through agent-led, zero-touch workflows that enable fully automated, end-to-end operations.
- **Link:** [Agent-Powered Zero-Touch](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/M25.0.832/agentenabled-autonomous-network-l4-dark-factory)

#### 8. Evolving to Full Network Autonomy
- **ID:** M25.0.792
- **Focus:** Level 5 autonomous network demonstration
- **Description:** Showcasing a fully autonomous Level 5 network leveraging enhanced observability, knowledge graphs, and AIOps-driven closed loops. Integrates AIOps, digital twins, and GenAI.
- **Link:** [Full Network Autonomy](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/M25.0.792/Evolving-to-full-network-autonomy)

#### 9. AI Agent Empowering Higher Autonomous Level
- **ID:** C24.0.672
- **Focus:** LLM-based AI Agent with network digital twin
- **Description:** Pioneers an LLM-based AI Agent utilizing a network digital twin to amalgamate insights from current systems. The agent autonomously executes complex tasks using AIOps.
- **Link:** [AI Agent Higher Autonomy](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/C24.0.672/ai-agent-empowering-higher-autonomous-level)

#### 10. AI-Driven Proactive Customer-Centric O&M
- **ID:** C25.5.868
- **Focus:** AN Level 4 via AI, agents, and digital twins
- **Description:** Delivers AN Level 4 autonomy via AI, agents, and digital twins — proactive, user-centric, automated O&M for superior experience and efficiency.
- **Link:** [AI-Driven O&M](https://www.tmforum.org/catalysts/projects/C25.5.868/AI-driven-proactive-Customer-Centric-O&M:-Empowered-by-multi-agent-and-digital-twin)

## Catalyst Award Winners (DTW Ignite 2025)

Agentic AI was among the winners in TM Forum Catalyst awards at DTW Ignite 2025. Winning projects implemented TM Forum Open APIs, rApps, and intent-based orchestration across service layers, building self-adaptive systems that integrate the Autonomous Networks Reference Architecture with O-RAN interfaces.
