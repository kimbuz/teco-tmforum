## Three Mission Summits (Pathways to Growth)

DTW Ignite 2026 is organized around three curated pathways, each addressing an industry mission vital for bringing the industry back to growth:

### 1. Composable IT and Ecosystems
- Building flexible, modular IT architectures
- Ecosystem partnerships and B2B2X services
- Platform business models
- Cloud-native, composable software architectures
- Open API adoption and interoperability

### 2. Autonomous Networks
- End-to-end autonomous network operations
- AI-driven automation and intent-based networking
- Level 4 and Level 5 autonomy demonstrations
- Self-healing, self-optimizing networks
- Agentic architectures for network management

### 3. AI and Data
- Safe adoption of AI and Data
- Generative AI and Agentic AI in telco
- AI governance and responsible deployment
- Data monetization strategies
- AI-native telco transformation
