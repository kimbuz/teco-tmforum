# TM Forum Publications & Reports — AI, Autonomous Networks, and Agentic AI

## Research Reports

### 1. Agentic AI and Autonomy: CSPs Set Out Their Strategies
- **Type:** Benchmark Report
- **Published:** 2025
- **Summary:** Explores how agentic AI is front and center of operators' strategies as a key enabler to greater network autonomy. Covers CSP perspectives on opportunities, use case prioritization, and deployment challenges.
- **Key Questions Addressed:**
  - What do CSPs think of agentic AI opportunities?
  - How is it changing their thinking about AI impact?
  - Which use cases are they prioritizing?
  - What challenges need addressing for scale deployment?
- **Link:** [Read Report](https://inform.tmforum.org/research-and-analysis/reports/agentic-ai-and-autonomy-csps-set-out-their-strategies)

### 2. Autonomous Networks Implementation Guide
- **Type:** Whitepaper
- **Published:** June 2025
- **Summary:** Provides a blueprint including strategy, processes, and architecture to reach Level 4 AN. Documents the shift from traditional human-defined automation to true autonomous decision-making.
- **Link:** [Read Report](https://inform.tmforum.org/research-and-analysis/reports/autonomous-networks-implementation-guide)

### 3. New-Generation Intelligent Operations: Agentic AI-Driven Transformation
- **Type:** Research Report
- **Published:** June 2025
- **Summary:** Examines how architectures and maturity models can leverage agentic AI, GenAI, digital twin networks, and service impact assessment systems. Enables CSPs to take advantage of new digital service opportunities.
- **Link:** [Read Report](https://inform.tmforum.org/research-and-analysis/reports/new-generation-intelligent-operations-agentic-ai-driven-transformation)

### 4. Optimizing Governance to Accelerate GenAI Deployment
