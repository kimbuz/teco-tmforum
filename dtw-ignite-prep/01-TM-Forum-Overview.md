# TM Forum — Complete Overview

## What is TM Forum?

TM Forum is a global industry association for telecommunications service providers and their suppliers. Founded in 1988 (originally as the OSI/Network Management Forum), it has grown into the largest international standardization organization in the telecom management field, with over **850 member organizations** spanning the global connectivity ecosystem.

### Members Include:
- Top 10 Communication Service Providers (CSPs) worldwide
- Top 3 hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Leading Network Equipment Providers (NEPs)
- Vendors, consultancies, and system integrators

### Mission
TM Forum is a global alliance actively working for industry-defining change that unlocks growth, long-term value, and winning conditions for all participants. They use collective influence and expertise to innovate, forge new frameworks, and standardize efficiency.

## Core Frameworks and Standards

### 1. Open Digital Architecture (ODA)
A blueprint to build flexible, cloud-based telecom and enterprise IT systems. ODA provides the foundation for operators to:
- Simplify enterprise architecture designs
- Modernize software for a plug-and-play ecosystem
- Automate IT and network operations
- Enable zero-touch interoperability

**Key characteristics:**
- Model- and data-driven architecture
- Cloud-native deployment
- Component-based with business services exposed via Open APIs
- Standardized, interoperable software components organized into loosely coupled domains

### 2. Open APIs
TM Forum maintains **60+ Open APIs** that are REST-based, technology-agnostic, and usable in any digital service scenario (B2B, IoT, Smart Health, NFV, Next-Gen OSS/BSS, etc.).

**Notable APIs include:**

| API ID | Name | Purpose |
|--------|------|---------|
| TMF620 | Product Catalog Management | Lifecycle management of catalog elements |
| TMF641 | Service Ordering Management | Standardized service order placement |
| TMF622 | Product Ordering | Product order management |
| TMF632 | Party Management | Customer/party data management |
| TMF638 | Service Inventory | Service inventory management |
| TMF639 | Resource Inventory | Resource inventory management |
| TMF645 | Service Qualification | Service qualification checks |
| TMF633 | Service Catalog | Service catalog management |

### 3. Business Process Framework (eTOM)
A comprehensive, industry-agreed, multi-layered view of the key business processes required to run an efficient and agile digital enterprise. It provides end-to-end business process guidelines for telecom operators.

### 4. Information Framework (SID)
Provides an information/data reference model along with a common vocabulary for implementing business processes. The model is independent of platform, language, or protocol and helps identify business entities important to CSPs.

### 5. Application Framework (TAM)
Defines the application landscape for telecom operators, mapping applications to business processes.

### 6. ODA Canvas
The runtime environment for ODA components — a cloud-native platform that orchestrates components using AI.

### 7. ODA Components
Reusable, plug-and-play software building blocks that perform IT and network functions, exposing their functionality through Open APIs.

## Key Programs and Initiatives

### Catalysts Program
Proof-of-concept projects where members collaborate to demonstrate innovative solutions. These are showcased at DTW events and drive real-world implementations.

### Autonomous Networks Project
A major initiative defining what is required to deliver true autonomous networks, including the use of GenAI and creating self-healing domains. (See dedicated document for details.)

### Open Digital Framework (ODF)
Supports the telecoms industry in creating agile solutions by inspiring new ways to implement services designed on autonomous AI-driven networks.

### Conformance Certification
Verifies successful implementation of Open APIs in commercial products and real-world deployments.

## How TM Forum Works

1. **Collaborative Projects** — Members work together in projects to develop standards and best practices
2. **Research & Publications** — Reports, whitepapers, and analysis through TM Forum Inform
3. **Events** — DTW Ignite (flagship), regional summits, workshops
4. **Catalysts** — Proof-of-concept innovation projects
5. **Training & Certification** — Upskilling programs and conformance certification
