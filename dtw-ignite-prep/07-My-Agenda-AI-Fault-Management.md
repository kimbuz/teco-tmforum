# DTW Ignite 2026 — Recommended Sessions: AI for Fault Management

Your curated agenda focused on AI-driven fault management, self-healing, root cause analysis, and autonomous operations.

---

## Priority 1: Must Attend

### Masterclass: Core Building Blocks of AN — AI-Powered Agents, Intent-Driven Automation, and Self-Healing Capabilities Enable Level 4 Autonomy
- **Type:** Masterclass (interactive, deep-dive)
- **Why:** Directly covers self-healing networks, AI agents for fault resolution, and how they detect/predict/resolve anomalies in real time. Includes case studies.
- **You'll learn:**
  - How intent-driven automation translates business goals into automated workflows
  - How AI-powered agents make decisions and execute without manual intervention
  - How self-healing logic detects, predicts, and autonomously resolves anomalies
- **Link:** [Session page](https://dtw.tmforum.org/agenda/masterclass-core-building-blocks-of-an-ai-powered-agents-intent-driven-automation-and-self-healing-capabilities-enable-level-4-autonomy-1932)

### How is AI Driving Zero-Touch Network Operations?
- **Type:** Conference session
- **Why:** Covers AI integration with legacy systems for fault management, self-healing for 5G and beyond, and real-time reliability optimization.
- **You'll learn:**
  - How AI-powered automation reduces operational overhead
  - How AI integrates with legacy systems (relevant for brownfield like ours)
  - How AI-driven insights optimize performance and ensure reliability in real-time
  - How AI enables self-healing and scalable solutions
- **Link:** [Session page](https://dtw.tmforum.org/agenda/how-is-ai-driving-zero-touch-network-operations-1828)

## Key Questions to Ask at Sessions

1. "What's the minimum data quality/completeness needed to start closed-loop fault management?"
2. "How do you handle the transition from rule-based alarm correlation to AI-driven RCA in a brownfield environment?"
3. "What's the role of the service model (CFS/RFS) in enabling impact analysis for self-healing?"
4. "How do you build trust in AI decisions for fault remediation — what guardrails exist?"
5. "What's the path from single-domain self-healing (L3) to cross-domain (L4)?"
